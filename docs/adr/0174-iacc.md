# ADR 0174: Interaural cross-correlation

Status: accepted, 2026-11-16. It is the binaural counterpart of the ISO 3382 energy parameters
(ADRs 0171 to 0173).

## Context

ISO 3382-1 measures spatial impression from a binaural impulse response pair `l` and `r`. The
measure is the normalized interaural cross-correlation, maximized over lags within ±1 ms:

    IACC = max_{|τ| ≤ 1 ms} |Σ l[n]·r[n+τ]| / √(Σ l² · Σ r²)

Implementations differ on four points:

- integer or interpolated lags;
- how the lag range is rounded;
- whether the lag is returned as well;
- the length rule.

## Decision

- **The API.** In `measurements`:
  - Python: `iacc(left, right, sample_rate, *, max_lag=0.001)`.
  - C#: `Measurements.Iacc(left, right, sampleRate, maxLag = 0.001)`.
  - Both take real responses and return the value only.
- **The lags.** Only integer lags `τ`, with `|τ| ≤ L = ⌊max_lag·fs + 1/2⌋`, the same rounding as
  ADRs 0170 and 0172. Lags as long as the responses or longer add nothing and are skipped.
  `max_lag = 0` gives the lag-0 correlation alone.
- **The correlation.** `R(τ) = Σ_n l[n]·r[n+τ]`, over the `n` where both samples exist, with
  `τ > 0` meaning that `right` lags. The result is `max_τ |R(τ)| / √(E_l·E_r)`, which lies in
  `[0, 1]` by Cauchy–Schwarz.
- **The windows.** The whole responses are used. `IACC_E` (0 to 80 ms) and `IACC_L` (from
  80 ms) are the caller's slices.
- **The rules.**
  - `left` and `right` are one-dimensional, real, finite, of equal non-zero length, and neither
    is all zero.
  - `sample_rate` is finite and positive.
  - `max_lag` is finite and `≥ 0`.
  - Anything else is `invalid_argument`.

## Consequences

- **The lag is not returned.** A caller who needs it computes the full cross-correlation
  themselves.
- **Vectors are exact hand sums.** They are worked in fractions, with the square root in
  50-digit `decimal`. The cases are:
  - a delayed copy, which gives exactly 1 when its delay is in range and less when it is not;
  - an inverted copy, which gives 1, because the absolute value is taken;
  - orthogonal pairs, which give 0;
  - rounding of the lag range;
  - the errors.
