# ADR 0157: MFCC

Status: accepted, 2026-11-16. It builds on the mel filterbank (ADR 0155) and the DCT-II
(ADR 0156).

## Context

Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients are the DCT of the log mel band energies of one frame.
Implementations differ on four points:

- the log: natural, `10·log10`, or `log10`;
- what a zero band does, which would give `−∞`;
- the DCT scaling;
- liftering, and whether `c_0` is kept.

## Decision

- **The API.**
  - Python: `mfcc(power_spectrum, filterbank, n_coefficients, *, floor=1e-10)`, in
    `spectral`.
  - C#: `Spectral.Mfcc(ReadOnlySpan<double> powerSpectrum, double[][] filterbank,
    int nCoefficients, double floor = 1e-10)`, returning `double[]`.
  - It takes one frame's one-sided power spectrum of `K` bins and a filterbank of
    `n_filters` rows of `K` weights, such as `mel_filterbank`. Keeping the two separate lets
    any filterbank and any spectrum estimate be used.
- **The computation.**
  1. Each band is `E_i = Σ_k W[i][k]·P[k]`.
  2. Each log is `L_i = ln(max(E_i, floor))`, a natural log.
  3. The result is the first `n_coefficients` values of the orthonormal `dct(L)`, with
     `c_0` kept.
- **The floor.** It clamps an empty or silent band to a finite log. It is an argument, so a
  caller who wants a zero band to stand out can pass a tiny value. It must be finite and
  positive.
- **No liftering.** It can be added later as an option without changing the default.
- **The rules.**
  - The power spectrum is 1-D, finite and non-negative, with at least one bin.
  - The filterbank is 2-D with at least one row. Every row has `K` weights that are finite
    and non-negative.
  - `1 ≤ n_coefficients ≤ n_filters`.
  - Anything else is `invalid_argument`.

## Consequences

- **Converting to dB.** Multiplying by `10/ln 10` gives dB-scaled coefficients, since the DCT
  is linear.
- **Vectors are hand-worked.** The DCT uses exact cosines for `N = 2, 3, 4`, and the logs are
  computed in 50-digit `decimal`. The cases are:
  - an identity filterbank on `[1, 2, 4]`, so the logs are `0, ln 2, 2 ln 2`;
  - equal bands, which leave only `c_0`;
  - truncation;
  - all-zero power, which hits the floor;
  - a custom floor;
  - overlapping triangles;
  - the errors.
- **Tests.**
  - A filterbank from `mel_filterbank` applied to a white spectrum.
  - Scaling the power by `a` changes only `c_0`, by `√N·ln a`.
